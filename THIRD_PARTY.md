## glimmer (MIT)

Parts of the UI, the notification ("attention") queue and the push-card contract are adapted from
**glimmer**, a desk-display firmware for the GeekMagic SmallTV-Ultra: <https://github.com/Avinava/glimmer>.
Files derived from it carry an `SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT` header and a pointer to this file. They live in
`include/glimmer/` and `src/glimmer/`, which are excluded from the clang-tidy style gate so they can stay close to
upstream. Code written for this project that builds on them is in `include/ui/`, `src/ui/` and `src/web/PushApi.cpp`.

## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

`data/fonts/*.vlw` are bitmap rasterisations (generated by glimmer's `tools/genfonts.py`) of these fonts, all
distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (<https://openfontlicense.org>):

- [VT323](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/VT323)
- [Silkscreen](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Silkscreen)
- [DM Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Mono)
- [Pixelify Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Pixelify+Sans)

## Certificates

`include/net/TrustAnchors.h` embeds public root and intermediate CA certificates (ISRG Root X1/X2 and the Let's Encrypt
"Gen Y" ECDSA intermediates YE1-YE3, published at <https://letsencrypt.org/certificates/>) used to verify claude.ai and
chatgpt.com. They are public trust anchors, not secrets.
